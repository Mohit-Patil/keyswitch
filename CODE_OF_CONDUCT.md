# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

KeySwitch should be a welcoming, practical, and respectful community. Project
maintainers, contributors, and participants are expected to make collaboration
safe for people of every background, identity, ability, and level of
experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with patience, empathy, and professional respect.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person proposing them.
- Give actionable feedback and accept it in good faith.
- Acknowledge mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from them.
- Protect private information found in logs, screenshots, or reports.
- Prioritize the health of the project and community over winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider unsafe or inappropriate in a
  professional open-source community.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents
the KeySwitch project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact details
on [Mohit Patil's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Mohit-Patil). Do not post
sensitive reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly,
impartially, and with respect for the reporter's privacy.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue a private
warning, limit participation, or permanently exclude a participant. The
response will be proportional to the behavior, its impact, and any established
pattern. Retaliation and repeated violations will lead to stronger action.

Maintainers who are directly involved in a report should recuse themselves
when another suitable reviewer is available.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the principles and enforcement model of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
with project-specific wording.
